Some of the hyperparameters of the random forest algorithm is here:

n_estimators -> No. of trees in the model.

max_depth -> maximum depth of a tree.

min_samples_split -> Minimum number of samples required to split an internal node.

min_samples_leaf  ->  Minimum number of samples allowed in a leaf. Increasing this forces 
                      leaves to contain more observations and can reduce overfitting.

max_features  -> Number/fraction of features considered when looking for a split.
                 This is particularly important for Random Forest because Random 
                 Forest's diversity comes partly from considering different subsets of features.
